CardioWave DSP is a synthetic twelve lead E C G workbench that runs the whole signal chain in one page.
Everything is computed in the browser, deterministically, with zero dependencies and no network.

A seeded generator builds the beat interval series with Mayer wave and respiration components.
Twelve coupled McSharry oscillators integrate on a fourth order Runge Kutta stepper, then project through a twelve by five amplitude matrix.
Mains hum, baseline wander and band limited E M G noise join per lead, while the ground truth beats stay known by construction.

One click generates and analyses a record, scoring two detectors against that truth.
A single lead two Pan Tompkins chain band passes, differentiates, squares and integrates over a one hundred twenty millisecond window.
A twelve lead voting ensemble clusters detections and refines each survivor on the spatial magnitude trace.
The stat grid reports sensitivity, precision, F one and timing R M S E at a one hundred fifty millisecond tolerance.

The signal tab draws all twelve leads, the Butterworth magnitude response, and the Pan Tompkins stages from filtered wave to detection envelope.
Back on the bench, a live Poincare plot marks each beat pair with its S D one S D two ellipse, and the rhythm card classifies the strip by deterministic rules.

H R V analysis reports time domain stats, Poincare geometry and a Welch power spectral density with L F and H F band powers.
The evaluation tab runs a nine case benchmark across rates, sampling rates and noise, measures the real filter response at six probe frequencies, and names seven known failure modes in the open.

The export tab writes PhysioNet W F D B compatible header, signal and annotation text, plus a clinical strip and an S H A 256 fingerprinted report.
Three hundred thirty six Node tests pass with zero failures, re verified inside this recording run.
